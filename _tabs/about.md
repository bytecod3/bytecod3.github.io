Hi. Welcome to my blog.

I’m an embedded systems engineer with a passion for designing reliable
firmware and embedded architectures from the ground up.
My interests span embedded software, electronics, and system design,
with projects ranging from amateur cube-sat systems and industrial automation to
communication protocols and low-level device drivers. <br> I enjoy translating system requirements
into robust implementations using sound engineering principles, whether that means designing
finite state machines, applying software design patterns, developing hardware abstraction layers,
or optimizing firmware for resource-constrained microcontrollers.


<p>
This blog is where I document the engineering behind my projects; the decisions, trade-offs,
debugging sessions, and lessons learned, my thoughts about this side of engineering and other topics. 
My intention is that that other embedded engineers can apply these ideas to their own work, and with the hope of learning from them too.


Right now, am on a quest to build an amateur cubesat, using an STM32, for educational and practise purpose.
You can read more about the project here: <a href="https://github.com/bytecod3/Project-Shadow-Flight" target="_blank">Project Shadow Flight</a>. The goal is to
come up with a working cubesat in a record period of time as I practise and master my system design skills.
