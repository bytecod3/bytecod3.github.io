Hi! I am Edwin. Welcome to my blog.

I’m a systems architect, embedded developer, and electronics engineer with a deep background in designing
robust electronics for consumer products, industrial embedded devices, and any device that deals with embedded systems.
<p>
My specialty is in first-stage low-level design and development using STM32, ESP32, Celullar modules, nRF systems, ultra-cheap 8-bit micro-controllers,  embedded linux, and CAD mechanical design. I handle the low-level interface to backend systems in IoT applications. Whether you need a quick, dirty BUT working prototype or a full fledged product you can sell, I can build all that, all the way to certification.

I have worked in R&D teams in companies and built successful industrial data acquisition systems(DAQs), AC load management systems, custom data loggers, induction cookers, electric vehicle chargers, remote payment modules and many more.
</p>

<p>
This blog is where I document the engineering behind my side projects; the decisions, trade-offs, successes, failures,
debugging sessions, and lessons learned, my thoughts about this side of engineering and other topics. 
My intention is that that other embedded engineers can apply these ideas to their own work, and with the hope of learning from them too.
</p>
